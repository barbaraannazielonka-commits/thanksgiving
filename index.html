Thanksgiving
Beyond the Dinner Table

One holiday. Many histories. Different perspectives.

Choose your level and explore what Thanksgiving means in the United States.

YOUR LEARNING GOALS

Lesson objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

A1–A2 · Explorer

  • Say when and how people celebrate Thanksgiving in the USA.
  • Name traditional Thanksgiving foods and ingredients in English.
  • Find key information in short reading and listening texts.
  • Explain in simple English why some people do not celebrate Thanksgiving.
  • Create a simple Thanksgiving menu using food vocabulary.

B2–C1 · Critical Thinker

  • Explain the historical background and contemporary significance of Thanksgiving.
  • Compare different perspectives, including Indigenous perspectives and reasons for boycotting the holiday.
  • Identify arguments and supporting evidence in written and spoken texts.
  • Use precise culinary and cultural vocabulary in context.
  • Design and justify a culturally respectful menu and reflect on the holiday using well-supported arguments.
